## What Dritchwear is

Dritchwear is a full-service merch partner for companies and events in Nigeria. We source the products, brand them with the client's logo, and deliver them.

> We find it, brand it and gift it.

Typical orders: onboarding kits for new employees, tech event and conference merch, team and corporate gifts, branded bulk orders.

We are not a single-item streetwear store. Retail pieces can still be sold, but every primary surface leads with merch for teams and events.

## Who we speak to

The buyer, not the wearer: HR and people teams, operations and office managers, event organizers, founders and marketing leads. They care about getting it right, getting it on time, and staying on budget.

## How we work (use this order everywhere)

1. **We find it.** The client tells us the item, quantity, budget and date. We source quality options.
2. **We brand it.** Their logo is applied locally. They approve a mockup or sample first.
3. **We gift it.** Packed and delivered in 3 to 5 days, to their office, their event, or straight to each recipient.

## Origin claims

We source products and brand them locally. We do not manufacture most items.

- Say: "Sourced, branded and delivered in Nigeria", "Branded in Nigeria", "Nigeria's merch partner for teams and events", "You bring the logo. We handle the rest."
- Never say: "Made in Nigeria", "Nigerian made", "locally produced", "manufactured by Dritchwear". The only exception is a specific item we actually produce ourselves, and only on that item.
- True local benefits we can lead with: branding done locally with mockup or sample approval, faster branding turnaround than ordering branded goods from abroad, naira pricing with no exchange rate surprises, a real local team, one partner for every item in a kit.

## Writing rules

- No dash punctuation (em or en dashes, or hyphens used as dashes) in product copy, emails or UI text.
- Lead with outcomes for the buyer: turnaround, quality, approval before production, delivery.
- Primary call to action is "Get a quote" or "Start your order", not "Add to cart".
- Show proof: case studies, client logos, real branded samples, turnaround times, minimum order quantity.
- Standard facts: minimum order 10 pieces; delivery in 3 to 5 days.

## Product principles

- Merch for teams and events is the primary journey: browse, request a quote, approve, pay, receive.
- Gift cards, Pay for Me, wallet, custom orders, rewards and bill payment remain available services, but sit behind the merch journey.
- Purple carries primary actions; gold highlights rewards and selected details.
- The web layout expands into a desktop storefront while retaining touch-friendly mobile controls.
