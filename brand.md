# Dritchwear Brand System

- Primary: `#5A2D82` - Dritchwear purple
- Accent: `#FDB813` - Dritchwear gold
- Interface font: Inter
- Voice: confident, direct, welcoming, fashion-forward

## Supporting palette

- Purple dark: `#32154E`
- Purple soft: `#F3EFF7`
- Gold soft: `#FFF6D8`
- Ink: `#17131C`
- Muted ink: `#665F6C`
- Canvas: `#F8F7F9`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Border: `#E8E3EB`

## Product principles

- Merchandise is the primary journey.
- Gift cards, Pay for Me, wallet, custom orders, rewards, and bill payment remain first-class services.
- Purple carries primary actions; gold highlights rewards and selected details.
- The web layout expands into a desktop storefront while retaining touch-friendly mobile controls.
